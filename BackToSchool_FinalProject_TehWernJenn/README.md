1. What is the creative motivation / intended use of your project.
(Why did you make it? Who do you envision using it? What do you want the audience to experience?)


I created this as a fun way to reminisce about studying for exams while also creating a gameplay style. This game aims to look fun adn peaceful from the surface abit hopeful with the note that is is going to be easy as the stages are very similar in the first two, and with the sounds that are brighter with cute sketchu imagery, but one realise it gets more annoying or agitating as you play, when one realises the multiple number of tasks they have to finish for their studies which will slowly feel like never ending as the stage goes by. At the same time at every stage of a game there is a mini test which gets harder and harder to complete for the final exam. However there is no logic in the tests just to confuse the player and make them feel hopeless and tired - like how we feel in exams. It is like doing english in a math exam where the questions are more of trying to confuse you then test your knowledge. Envisioning people being forced to do what they dont want to do just to get to the end, its a game to for fun the might make you annoyed, bored, sturggle!
There is not much of guidance on how to play and one learns the game by exploring seeing what they can click/not and using common game techniques like clicking and dragging.
